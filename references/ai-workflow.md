# AI Thumbnail Workflow

## Best use of AI

AI is particularly useful for:

- concept generation
- variation generation
- expression exploration
- background alternatives
- subject isolation
- composition exploration
- feedback
- rapid iteration

## Hybrid workflow

A robust workflow is:

1. one-page brief
2. 5–8 concepts
3. quick screening
4. 2–3 final candidates
5. controlled variants
6. strategic/human review
7. publication
8. measurement
9. diagnosis
10. next iteration

## Standardized brief

Include:

- final title
- one-sentence viewer goal
- brand colors
- optimized colors when intentionally different
- font style
- logo rules
- primary subject
- emotional goal
- desired curiosity gap
- target format

## AI feedback loop

When reviewing an output, ask:

- What is the intended focal point?
- What is the predicted first fixation?
- What competes with it?
- Does the text help?
- Does the concept work on mobile?
- Does the title complement it?
- What single change would most improve it?

## Important

Do not accept AI output merely because it looks polished. Treat it as a hypothesis and verify the communication logic.
