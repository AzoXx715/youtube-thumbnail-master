# Quality Control

## Composition

- one dominant idea
- one clear focal point
- no accidental tangencies
- intentional negative space
- no competing visual center

## Typography

- short text
- correct spelling
- strong contrast
- readable at small size
- no unnecessary decoration

## Subjects and faces

- realistic anatomy
- correct hands
- believable expression
- clean silhouette
- no accidental distortion

## Objects

- plausible geometry
- consistent perspective
- realistic contact points
- coherent scale

## Lighting

- believable direction
- coherent shadows
- highlights support the subject
- no unexplained light sources

## Background

- contextual
- simplified
- subordinate
- free of accidental focal points

## AI artifact scan

Look for:

- extra fingers
- fused fingers
- warped faces
- asymmetrical eyes
- broken teeth
- floating objects
- unreadable text
- impossible reflections
- broken geometry
- inconsistent shadows

## Final mobile check

At small scale:

- Can I tell what matters?
- Can I read the hook?
- Can I understand the basic story?
- Does anything irrelevant attract me first?

If not, simplify and retest.
