# Mobile Optimization

## Why it matters

YouTube thumbnails are often encountered at small sizes. A thumbnail that only works when zoomed in is not robust.

## Tests

Evaluate:

- full resolution
- 25% scale
- approximately 10% scale
- approximately 120px-wide equivalent

## Pass conditions

At small size:

- primary subject remains obvious
- basic emotional signal remains visible
- text, if used, remains readable
- visual story survives
- irrelevant background detail disappears into the background

## Fixes when it fails

Prefer:

- larger subject
- shorter text
- stronger separation
- simpler background
- fewer objects
- stronger silhouette
- clearer crop

Avoid compensating with endless effects.
