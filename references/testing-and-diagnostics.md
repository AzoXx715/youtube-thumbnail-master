# Testing and Diagnostics

## Principle

A thumbnail is a hypothesis.

Use real-world evidence to evaluate the hypothesis.

## Metrics

Consider:

- impressions
- CTR
- watch time
- early retention
- broader viewer satisfaction signals when available

## Controlled testing

When practical, modify one major variable at a time.

Examples:

- face expression
- subject size
- hook wording
- crop
- background complexity
- supporting object

## Diagnostic matrix

### Attention aligned + weak CTR

Investigate:

- appeal
- curiosity
- title-thumbnail relationship
- audience relevance
- differentiation

### Attention misaligned + weak CTR

Investigate:

- clutter
- background competition
- weak subject
- contrast
- composition

### Strong CTR + weak retention

Investigate:

- expectation mismatch
- exaggeration
- misleading packaging
- weak opening relative to promise

### Inconclusive results

Do not overinterpret noisy data. Collect more evidence or run a cleaner test.

## Important

A higher CTR is not inherently proof of a better overall package. A useful thumbnail must attract the right viewer and set an expectation the video can satisfy.
