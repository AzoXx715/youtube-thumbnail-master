# Title + Thumbnail System

## Principle

The title and thumbnail are two pieces of one packaging system.

Neither needs to explain everything alone.

## Complementarity

Useful pairings:

- title explains event → thumbnail emphasizes result
- title explains result → thumbnail emphasizes conflict
- title explains method → thumbnail emphasizes outcome
- title asks question → thumbnail visually intensifies uncertainty

## Avoid

- exact repetition
- thumbnail that makes the title irrelevant
- title that makes the thumbnail irrelevant
- visual promises the video cannot support

## Curiosity gap

The pair should answer:

"What is this about?"

while leaving a meaningful:

"But what happens next?"

The gap should be interesting, not confusing.
