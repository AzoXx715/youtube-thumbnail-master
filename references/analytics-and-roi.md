# Thumbnail Analytics and ROI

## Metrics

Track when available:

- impressions
- CTR
- views
- watch time
- average view duration
- early retention
- watch time per impression
- traffic source
- audience/device breakdown
- production time
- production cost

## Why CTR is incomplete

A thumbnail earns the click; the video must satisfy the expectation.

High CTR + weak retention can indicate poor packaging alignment.

Lower CTR + strong retention can indicate a packaging opportunity without a content-quality problem.

## Time windows

Useful checkpoints can include:

- first 24 hours
- 7 days
- 14 days
- 28 days

Use windows appropriate to channel volume and topic velocity.

## Baselines

Compare against:

- the video's own historical baseline
- similar videos on the same channel
- same traffic source
- similar audience
- similar topic

Avoid universal thresholds.

## ROI

When evaluating AI/manual/hybrid workflows, consider:

**incremental performance value − design cost − time cost**

Track both creative performance and production efficiency.

## Experiment logging

For each test log:

- video
- control
- variant
- exact change
- start/end
- impressions
- CTR
- watch time
- retention
- traffic source
- conclusion
- next hypothesis

The long-term asset is the accumulated evidence base.
