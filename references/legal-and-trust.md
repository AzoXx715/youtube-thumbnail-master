# Legal, Rights and Viewer Trust

## Accuracy

The thumbnail must be an honest representation of the video.

Do not:

- imply an event that did not happen
- fabricate a result
- use a face that changes the meaning
- use an unrelated shocking image
- make a false before/after

## Assets

For external images, verify:

- license
- commercial-use permission
- attribution requirements
- source
- modifications allowed

Keep a simple asset record.

## Celebrity and third-party faces

Do not assume a public photo is free to use commercially.

When rights are unclear, prefer:

- owned images
- licensed assets
- original artwork
- properly permitted likenesses

## AI identity

Do not copy another creator's face or distinctive identity.

AI should support original creative work, not impersonation.

## Viewer trust

A strong thumbnail should attract the intended viewer and set an expectation the opening of the video can satisfy.

The optimization target is not the maximum possible click at any cost. It is qualified attention that can turn into satisfied viewing.
