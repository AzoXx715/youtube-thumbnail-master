# Video Source and AI Editing

## Frame extraction

When a video is available, inspect candidate frames rather than automatically selecting a random frame.

Look for:

- clear subject
- strong pose
- clean expression
- useful action
- unusual moment
- good lighting
- readable silhouette
- minimal clutter

Score several keyframes per scene when tooling allows.

## Edit the frame

After selecting a source frame, improve:

- crop
- subject isolation
- background
- lighting
- contrast
- color
- expression when legitimate
- text

Do not fabricate a promise the video does not contain.

## AI editing

AI can assist with:

- background removal
- relighting
- cleanup
- object replacement
- face/identity consistency when authorized
- controlled expression variations
- compositing

Always run technical QA after AI edits.

## Identity and assets

Only use faces, logos, photographs, and other assets when the creator has the required rights or permission.

Do not clone another creator's identity or distinctive visual identity.
