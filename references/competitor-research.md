# Competitor Research

## Objective

Use competitors to discover patterns worth testing, not to copy individual thumbnails.

## Sampling

Prefer:

- 3–5 relevant competitors for focused analysis
- recent uploads
- comparable formats
- comparable topics
- separate Shorts and long-form
- same or similar audience context

Avoid relying on:

- lifetime views alone
- one viral outlier
- generic CTR benchmarks
- raw subscriber count without context

## Baselines

For each channel, track when available:

- recent views
- median views
- uploads per month
- 24–72h velocity
- engagement
- subscriber conversion
- CTR/retention when legitimately available
- traffic-source context

## Outlier method

Identify videos that substantially outperform that channel's own baseline.

Then inspect:

- title formula
- thumbnail composition
- subject scale
- expression
- text hook
- color treatment
- topic
- opening hook
- traffic sources

Look for patterns repeated across multiple successful videos.

## Output

Turn observations into hypotheses such as:

- larger face + shorter hook
- single-object composition
- stronger subject/background separation
- result-focused visual
- comparison layout

Then test those hypotheses on the user's own content.

## Do not copy

Never reproduce a competitor's exact:

- composition
- wording
- branding
- visual assets
- distinctive creative concept

Extract the principle and adapt it.
