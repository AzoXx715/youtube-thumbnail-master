# Thumbnail Brand System

## Principle

**Repeat the brand, change the story.**

Branding should make a channel recognizable without forcing every thumbnail into the same composition.

## Fixed cues

Potentially stabilize:

- core palette
- headline font
- typography treatment
- capitalization
- logo treatment
- image treatment
- recurring subject/text zones

## Variable cues

Adapt per video:

- subject
- background
- expression
- hook
- supporting object
- secondary color
- composition when the topic requires it

## Category templates

Use multiple templates when topics have genuinely different visual structures. A tutorial, breaking-news video, product review, and story video do not need identical layouts.

## Brand audit

Before publishing, compare the candidate beside 6–12 recent uploads.

Check:

- instant channel recognition
- sufficient variation
- readable typography
- consistent image treatment
- no visual fatigue
- no overuse of one composition

## Important caution

Branding is a design system, not a promise of higher CTR. Use actual channel data to validate whether a repeated cue helps recognition or performance.
