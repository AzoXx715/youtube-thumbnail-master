# Workflow: Competitor Thumbnail Analysis

1. Select 3–5 relevant competitors.
2. Collect 10–20 recent comparable thumbnails per channel.
3. Separate formats and traffic contexts.
4. Establish each channel's own baseline.
5. Identify outliers.
6. Annotate subject, face/no-face, text, hook, color, contrast, composition, and visual metaphor.
7. Find repeated patterns across successful outliers.
8. Separate topic effects from thumbnail effects where possible.
9. Convert patterns into 3–5 original hypotheses.
10. Build test variants for the user's own channel.
11. Record outcomes and update the channel-specific design system.

Do not copy exact creative work.
