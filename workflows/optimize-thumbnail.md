# Workflow: Optimize Thumbnail

## Objective

Improve the thumbnail while preserving what already works.

## Step 1: Identify the current hypothesis

State the current:

- focal point
- visual promise
- emotional trigger
- curiosity gap
- title relationship

## Step 2: Diagnose

Classify the main issue as one or more of:

- hierarchy
- readability
- clutter
- weak subject
- weak curiosity
- weak differentiation
- title mismatch
- expectation mismatch
- technical quality

## Step 3: Protect strengths

Do not rebuild successful elements without reason.

Examples:

- strong face + weak background → keep the face, simplify background
- clear hierarchy + weak hook → preserve composition, revise hook
- strong CTR + retention issue → investigate promise rather than simply adding visual intensity

## Step 4: Build controlled variants

Create variants with a single major hypothesis:

A. subject scale
B. expression
C. hook text
D. supporting object
E. background simplicity
F. crop/composition

## Step 5: Compare

Explain:

- what changed
- what should change in viewer attention
- what evidence would validate the change

Never present a predicted CTR as guaranteed.
