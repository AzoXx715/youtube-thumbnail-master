# Workflow: Analyze Thumbnail

## Objective

Diagnose the thumbnail rather than merely describing it.

## Inspect

### 1. Focal point

Identify the intended first fixation.

### 2. Actual attention competition

Look for:

- brightest region
- strongest contrast
- largest shape
- sharpest details
- faces
- logos
- text
- directional lines

### 3. Hierarchy

Determine whether attention plausibly flows:

**subject → hook → support → background**

### 4. Mobile

Assess at reduced scale.

Check:

- subject recognition
- expression recognition
- text legibility
- silhouette
- visual story

### 5. Text

Assess:

- word count
- readability
- contrast
- semantic strength
- duplication of title

### 6. Background

Assess:

- clutter
- competing objects
- bright regions
- accidental focal points

### 7. Packaging

Assess:

- what the thumbnail communicates
- what the title communicates
- whether they complement each other
- what question remains after seeing both

### 8. Technical QA

Check anatomy, objects, perspective, lighting, typography, crop, and rendering artifacts.

## Output

Provide:

- strengths
- weaknesses
- attention path
- biggest distraction
- highest-impact fix
- optional micro-fixes
- 2–3 controlled test ideas

Do not use a simplistic single score as the only conclusion.
