# Workflow: Audience-Specific Thumbnail

1. Identify audience intent.
2. Identify traffic source.
3. Identify device context.
4. Identify geography/language if relevant.
5. Decide whether the thumbnail needs face/no-face.
6. Decide text/no-text.
7. Build the visual promise.
8. Check the concept against audience expectations without becoming generic.
9. Create one controlled variant for the most uncertain assumption.
10. Evaluate CTR and retention by the same context.

Never apply a global thumbnail rule when the evidence suggests the audience or traffic source changes the result.
